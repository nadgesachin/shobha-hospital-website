As of 23 Oct 2026 · Prepared for Suvacha Healthcare, Khanvel

## 1. Summary

The goal is for Suvacha to be the clinic Google shows, and its AI quotes, when someone near Khanvel or Silvassa searches for a gynecologist, pregnancy care, normal delivery, infertility or Panchakarma. The new website is built, optimised and live at [suvacha-healthcare.netlify.app](https://suvacha-healthcare.netlify.app). But two things still block ranking: the domain suvachahealthcare.in still shows the old WordPress site, and the Google listing is named "Shobha Hospital Khanvel" with no reviews.

The three things that matter most, in order:

1. **Move the domain to the new site.** Google cannot rank or quote pages it cannot see on the real domain.
2. **Fix and grow the Google Business Profile.** Use one consistent name, the right categories and a steady flow of genuine reviews. In local searches, Google's AI answers lean on this profile more than on any website.
3. **Publish answer-first content reviewed by our doctors.** Google is cautious with health topics, so it quotes sources that show clear medical expertise.

No one can guarantee the #1 position. Local results also depend on the searcher's distance from the clinic. This plan maximises everything we control.

## 2. How Google's AI search chooses sources

Google says AI Overviews and AI Mode follow the same rules as normal search. There is no special "AI SEO" trick. A page must be indexed and eligible to show with a snippet, and then the usual SEO best practices apply ([Google: AI features and your website](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features)).

Four facts shape our plan:

- **The AI runs many searches for one question.** Google calls this "query fan-out": it searches related subtopics and picks supporting pages for each. A page that clearly answers one specific question ("When should I see a gynecologist for irregular periods?") is more likely to be picked than a vague page.
- **Local answers come from the Business Profile.** Google ranks local results on relevance, distance and prominence. Prominence includes reviews and links from other websites ([Google Business Profile Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091?hl=en)).
- **Health is a "Your Money or Your Life" topic.** Google gives extra weight to experience, expertise, authoritativeness and trust (E-E-A-T) for health content ([Google: creating helpful content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content)).
- **The listing name must match real life.** The Business Profile name should be the name used on the signboard, website and stationery ([Google Business Profile guidelines](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177?hl=en)).

What this means for Suvacha:

| What Google's AI looks at | What we do |
| --- | --- |
| Is the page indexed on the real domain? | Point suvachahealthcare.in to the new site; submit the sitemap in Search Console |
| Does a page answer the exact question asked? | Answer-first pages, 32 service FAQs, focused articles |
| Is the source medically trustworthy? | Doctor names and qualifications, "Medically reviewed by", NHS/WHO sources |
| Is the clinic relevant and close to the searcher? | Business Profile with correct categories, services, hours and location |
| Is the clinic well known? | Genuine reviews, directory listings, local links |
| Are the facts the same everywhere? | One name, address and phone (NAP) on the website, Google and directories |

## 4. Google Business Profile plan

The Business Profile is the most important ranking factor for "near me" and local AI answers. Today the listing is named "Shobha Hospital Khanvel" and has no reviews, while the website is "Suvacha Healthcare".

### Decide the listing name

Choose one option:

- **Option A – Suvacha runs inside Shobha Hospital.** Create a separate profile for "Suvacha Healthcare" at the same address. Add a signboard for Suvacha at the entrance, because Google checks the real-world name.
- **Option B – Suvacha and Shobha Hospital are the same business.** Rename the existing profile to "Suvacha Healthcare", but only once the building signage shows that name.

Either way, Dr. Sachin Ladak Shedad can also have his own practitioner profile, as Google's guidelines allow for doctors.

### Set up the profile

- **Primary category:** Obstetrician-gynecologist. **Additional categories:** only the few that describe the core work, e.g. Gynecologist, Fertility clinic, Ayurvedic clinic.
- **Services:** add all 9 services with a one-line description each.
- **Hours:** Mon–Fri 8:00 AM – 7:00 PM, Sat 10:00 AM – 5:00 PM, Sun 10:00 AM – 2:00 PM.
- **Phone:** +91 88677 61598. **Website:** https://suvachahealthcare.in
- **Photos:** at least 10 real photos (signboard, entrance, reception, consultation room, doctors, team). Add a few new ones every month.
- **Posts:** one post a week, e.g. a health tip that links to a website article, or a Garbhasanskar batch announcement.

### Reviews

- Ask every satisfied patient for a review, in person and by WhatsApp after the visit.
- Put a QR code at reception that opens the review form.
- Reply to every review within 48 hours, without sharing any medical details.
- Never offer gifts or discounts for reviews; Google's policies forbid it.
- Target: a steady 5–10 genuine new reviews every month.

WhatsApp message to send after a visit:

> Namaste! Thank you for visiting Suvacha Healthcare. If you were happy with your care, a short Google review would help other families find us: [review link]. Get well soon!

## 5. Content plan for AI search

Google's AI quotes pages that answer a question clearly and come from a trusted medical source.

### How every page should be written

1. **Answer first.** The first two sentences answer the question directly.
2. **Then explain.** Use short sections with clear headings and lists.
3. **Say when to see a doctor.** Give specific warning signs and timelines.
4. **Show who wrote it.** Add the doctor's name, qualification and "Medically reviewed by".
5. **Link to trusted sources.** Use NHS, WHO or FOGSI pages.

## 6. Technical launch steps

1. **Back up the old WordPress site** (files, database, any form entries and email settings).
2. **Add the domain in Netlify:** suvachahealthcare.in and www.suvachahealthcare.in.
3. **Change DNS at the domain registrar:** an A record for @ pointing to 75.2.60.5, and a CNAME record for www pointing to suvacha-healthcare.netlify.app ([Netlify DNS guide](https://docs.netlify.com/manage/domains/configure-domains/configure-external-dns/)). Do not touch MX records if email runs on this domain.
4. **HTTPS:** Netlify issues the certificate automatically once DNS is live.
5. **Google Search Console:** verify the domain with a DNS TXT record, submit https://suvachahealthcare.in/sitemap.xml, and request indexing for the home page and the 9 service pages.
6. **Bing Webmaster Tools:** import the site from Search Console.
7. **Check old URLs:** confirm the old links open the new pages and nothing important returns 404.
8. **Monitor weekly:** Search Console coverage and performance.
